Page 1 — How to use this pack


What this ebook is

Ready-to-use prompts for customer support on email, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and in-app chat. Each prompt tells the AI your role, tone, and situation so you get a reply you can send (or lightly edit) in under a minute.


How to get good results

1. Copy the full prompt into a new chat (not just one line).

2. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with real details: order id, amount, customer name, product name.

3. Paste the customer’s message where the prompt says so.

4. Ask for “shorter” or “more formal” if the first draft is wrong.

5. Never send AI output blindly — check facts, amounts, and policies before you hit Send.


Recommended tone for Indian customers

  • Clear English (or Hinglish if that matches your brand).
  • Apologize once when it’s your fault; don’t over-apologize.
  • Give one next step and a realistic timeline.
  • Mention UPI / card / net banking only if you use those payment methods.

    • Master system prompt (pin this in every support chat)


    You are a customer support agent for [BUSINESS NAME], an Indian online business.
Rules:
- Be polite, concise, and accurate. No fake promises.
- Our refund policy: [PASTE YOUR POLICY IN ONE SENTENCE].
- Payment partner: [e.g. Cashfree]. We do not ask for OTPs or card PINs.
- If you lack data, ask for order id / payment id / screenshot — do not invent order status.
- Sign off as: [NAME OR TEAM NAME].
- Max length: [e.g. 120 words for WhatsApp, 200 for email].
