Page 1 — What this competition is


kamalvscockroach.com is a live, public pixel canvas competition. Two teams fight for territory:


  • Team Kamal (orange) — “Build the nation”
  • Team Cockroach (green) — “Spread the rot”

    • You buy pixels for ₹50 each. When payment succeeds, your pixel is marked sold and its colour is revealed on the canvas. There is no physical product — only digital placement on a 1,000×1,000 grid per side.


    The competition runs until the published deadline (see the countdown on the site). After that, new purchases close.


    What “winning” means: The team with more pixels sold leads the public progress bar. You are not buying equity or property — you are joining a viral, time-limited stunt.



    Page 2 — Choose your side strategically


    Team Kamal — when to pick orange

  • You want the “builder / nation” narrative.
  • Your audience is patriotic, earnest, or meme-friendly in that direction.
  • Orange looks strong in screenshots on light backgrounds.

    • Team Cockroach — when to pick green

  • You want the chaos / villain / joke energy.
  • Your group likes underdog or “agent of chaos” framing.
  • Green pops on dark-mode feeds.

    • Can you switch sides later?

    No. Each purchase is locked to the canvas you paid on. Plan your side before you pay.


    Single pixel vs bulk

    ModeBest for
    **Single**You want a exact spot (coordinate) or a personal “my pixel” story
    **Bulk**You want volume fast; random unsold pixels are assigned


    Page 3 — Single pixel: step by step


    1. Open the site on Chrome or Safari (avoid in-app browsers inside Instagram if checkout fails).

    2. Pick Kamal or Cockroach canvas.

    3. Tap an unsold pixel (not already marked sold).

    4. Confirm price ₹50.

    5. Pay via Cashfree (UPI, card, net banking).

    6. Wait for success — your pixel reveals colour on the live canvas.

    7. Open the purchase receipt and share your victory card.


    Tip: Screenshot the canvas with your pixel highlighted before and after — great for Instagram.



    Page 4 — Bulk pixels: step by step


    1. Choose your team canvas.

    2. Open bulk buy and enter how many pixels you want (within what’s still available).

    3. Complete payment once for the total (₹50 × count).

    4. The system assigns random unsold coordinates — you do not pick each one in bulk mode.

    5. Receipt lists your pixels (or a sample on the share card).


    Bulk is for impact: Moving the progress bar and flexing team totals — not for a precise logo drawing (use many single buys for that, if pixels remain).



    Page 5 — Reading the live scoreboard


    On the home page you will see:


  • “The Competition is Live” — purchases still open.
  • Progress bar — orange vs green share of total sold on both canvases.
  • Counts on each side — how many pixels that team has sold.
  • “Currently winning” — whichever team has more sold pixels.

    • Strategy: If your team trails, bulk buys near the deadline create a visible comeback narrative. If you lead, buys still cement the win in screenshots.



    Page 6 — Payments that work (India)


  • Use UPI inside Cashfree when possible — fastest for most users.
  • If money is debited but pixels don’t appear: note payment id / order id, email support with screenshot — do not pay twice immediately.
  • Do not share OTP, UPI PIN, or card CVV with anyone claiming to be “support.”
  • Cash on delivery is not available.

    • Keep your Cashfree confirmation until the competition ends.

    Page 8 — Common mistakes


    1. Buying in Instagram’s browser — checkout may fail; open site in Chrome/Safari.

    2. Clicking a sold pixel — choose grey/unmarked cells only.

    3. Paying twice when first payment is slow — wait 2 minutes, refresh, check canvas.

    4. Expecting a refund because you picked the “wrong” side — sales are final (see site refund policy).

    5. Spamming fake payment screenshots — only wastes your time; orders verify automatically.
