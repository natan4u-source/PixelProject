Instagram Growth for a Viral Stunt Site

Posts, Reels, and captions for competition-style launches


By Ninetyone Software Solutions

Version 1.0 — May 2026



Page 1 — Why Instagram fits stunt sites


Competition sites like kamalvscockroach.com win on visual proof and team identity:


  • Before/after canvas screenshots
  • Victory purchase cards
  • Countdown urgency
  • Orange vs green brand colours

    • Instagram rewards simple images + strong caption + one link. You do not need a huge follower count if shares in WhatsApp groups do the work.


    This ebook is a playbook, not a guarantee of virality. Use it for any “vs” or countdown campaign.



    Page 2 — Profile setup (30 minutes)


    Bio formula:

    Live pixel war · Pick a side
₹50 = 1 pixel on the canvas
👇 Join before deadline
[link]

    Link in bio: Use the site URL. Update bio when countdown hits “last 48 hours.”


    Highlight covers: 3 circles — How it works | Team Kamal | Team Cockroach.


    Pinned post: Explainer carousel (see Page 3).



    Page 3 — Carousel post (5 slides)


    Slide 1: “Two teams. One million pixels. Who wins?”


    Slide 2: Screenshot of home page with both canvases blurred slightly + arrow “Pick a side.”


    Slide 3: “₹50 → your pixel unlocks on the live map.”


    Slide 4: Example victory share card (your own purchase looks best).


    Slide 5: “Competition ends [DATE]. Link in bio.”


    Caption:

    It’s live. Kamal vs Cockroach on a public pixel canvas.
Buy a pixel. Your colour shows up instantly.
I’m on Team [X] — which side are you?
Link in bio · ₹50/pixel


    Page 4 — Reels scripts (15–30 sec)


    Reel A — “I bought one pixel”

  • Hook text on screen: “I spent ₹50 on a pixel war”
  • Screen record: tap pixel → pay → reveal
  • End: victory card + “link in bio”

    • Reel B — Countdown

  • Green/orange timer from site
  • Voiceover: “48 hours left”
  • CTA: comment “KAMAL” or “ROACH”

    • Reel C — Leaderboard drama

  • Screen record progress bar shifting
  • Text: “Cockroach is ahead” (update honestly)
  • CTA: “Shift the bar — link in bio”

    • Audio: Trending sound at low volume; don’t drown voice.



    Page 5 — Story sequence (7 frames)


    1. Poll: Kamal vs Cockroach

    2. Screenshot canvas close-up

    3. “₹50 = 1 pixel” sticker text

    4. Screen recording payment success

    5. Victory card with @mention invite

    6. Countdown sticker from site

    7. Link sticker to site


    Post stories daily in the last week.



    Page 6 — Caption templates (copy-paste)


    Urgency:

    Last [X] days of the pixel war.

    Social proof:

    CTA soft:

Kamal or Cockroach?


    Page 7 — Hashtags & discovery


    Core: #KamalVsCockroach #PixelWar #ViralIndia #InternetChallenge


    Optional: #StartupIndia #MemeMarketing #UGC #TeamKamal #TeamCockroach


    Rule: 5–8 hashtags max on feed posts; stories don’t need many.


    Geo (if relevant): #India #Bharat #DesiTwitter crossover posts


    Engage comments in first hour after posting — algorithm boost.



    Page 8 — WhatsApp + Instagram combo


    Instagram creates awareness; WhatsApp closes the sale.


    1. Post Reel

    2. Share Reel to family groups with one line: “₹50 joke gift”

    3. Reply privately with link when someone asks “how?”

    4. Post their victory card when they buy (with permission) — social proof loop


    Admin tip: Pre-write 3 reply templates in Notes app to paste quickly.

    Do not fabricate scores — use real screenshots from the site.



    Page 10 — Metrics that matter


    Track weekly:


  • Link clicks (Instagram insights)
  • Pixels sold (your admin or public counts)
  • Cost per pixel if you run ads (optional)
  • Saves & shares on best post

    • Ignore: Random likes from unrelated countries with no link clicks.


    If ads: Start ₹500/day targeting India 18–34, interest memes / gaming / startups. Kill ad if no clicks in 3 days.
